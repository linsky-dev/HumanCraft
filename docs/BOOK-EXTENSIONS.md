# Additional theory-to-engineering mapping

Independent operational interpretation, not a substitute for the original books. Source: Donald Norman, Design Psychology volumes 1–4, Chinese edition; volume and chapter locators below. This pass targets previously under-specified concepts; it is not a claim of a second complete line-by-line reading.

| Source | Design decision | Testable contract |
|---|---|---|
| Vol 1 ch 2–5: action cycle, external knowledge, constraints and errors | Show active target and state; distinguish habitual mistakes from mistaken plans; protect drafts and risky actions | HC-B01/B02/B03; alternate modes, interrupt, verify actual writes |
| Vol 2 ch 6: service systems and service blueprints | Map user steps, visible frontstage, backstage responsibility, dependencies and handoff; include staff as users | HC-B05/B09; no unassigned approval, transfer with context |
| Vol 2 ch 7: waiting experience | Make wait understandable; show real phase and policy; offer useful optional parallel work and an accurate ending | HC-B04/B08; stall, fail, retry, do not invent ETA or fairness claims |
| Vol 3 ch 3 and 5: behavior, reflection and trust | Align promise and delivered behavior; respect different contexts and avoid attention capture; provide meaningful recovery | HC-B08/B11/B12; failure after success, alert bursts, reversible preferences |
| Vol 4 ch 2, 5–6: shared context, augmented automation, natural communication | Share goal, scope, status and uncertainty; control notification urgency; provide real intervention | HC-B07/B11; cancel before commit, wait for late result, verify actual state |

Original extensions: independent root-cause diagnosis, behavioral mutation testing, evidence rubric, human research and four-arm model evaluation. These are engineering adaptations, not original Norman terminology or scientifically validated scales.
